Common questions

Bulk pricing FAQ

Do discounts apply across categories in one order?

Yes — mix textbooks, notebooks, pens and art supplies in a single order and the combined unit count determines your tier.

Is there a separate rate for schools with a tie-up?

Schools with an active tie-up get the 500+ institutional tier automatically, regardless of individual parent order size, plus dedicated booklist matching.

How fast can I get a formal quotation?

Standard bulk quotes are returned within 24 hours; full school tie-up booklists within 48 hours.

Can bulk orders be split across delivery dates?

Yes, large institutional orders can be phased across delivery dates at no extra cost — just mention this in your enquiry.

Why the tiers stay simple

Volume should change the rate, not the conversation.

The pricing model is designed so a larger requirement moves into the appropriate tier automatically. That means your team can plan quantities without starting a fresh negotiation for every order.
