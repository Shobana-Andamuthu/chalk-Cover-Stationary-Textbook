Quick answers

How soon will I hear back?

Bulk order enquiries get a reply within 24 hours, and school partnership enquiries within 48 hours — usually sooner during weekday store hours.

Do I need to visit the store to place a bulk or school order?

No — everything can be handled over email, WhatsApp or the forms above. A store visit is only needed if you'd like to check samples in person.

Can I track an order I've already placed?

Yes, order status and delivery updates are available from your account under My Orders, or you can ask our order desk directly with your order number.

What if my question isn't covered here?

Call or WhatsApp us at +91 98765 43210, or use the general enquiry form above — our team responds to every message personally.
